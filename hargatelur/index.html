Egg prices don't mean much on their own — $8.12 in Switzerland and $1.40 in Pakistan only make sense against what people earn. This ledger lines up egg prices, minimum monthly wages, and the resulting buying power (dozens of eggs a full month's minimum wage can buy) across 79 countries, colored by continent.
